How the floor's numbers work

In short: right now they're made up. Here's exactly how.

This is a demo. The trades, wins, and P&L on the mock floor are random. They don't reflect real markets, real model skill, or anything that would happen with real money.
1

Trades are random guesses

A worker picks a market and invents a number near its price (price plus random wiggle). If that number lands far enough from the price, it raises a "trade." There's no real thinking — it's noise.

2

Win or lose is a loaded coin flip

A few seconds after you approve a trade, it "resolves" by a coin flip. The coin is weighted per model — not by anything they actually did:

Claude wins 56% Kimi K2 wins 61% GLM-5.2 wins 43%

I picked those numbers so the floor looks alive and one book clearly loses. That's all.

3

The money math is the only real part

If a trade wins you gain contracts × (1 − price); if it loses you drop the whole stake. Fees use Kalshi's real formula. But since the win itself is fake, the P&L is fake too.

What "LIVE" mode does differently

Flip the floor to LIVE and the guess comes from the real models reading real Kalshi prices, and approved trades wait for the real market to settle — no coin flips. That's the real thing; the mock is just to test the buttons.
